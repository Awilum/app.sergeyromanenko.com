Privacy Policy

How the applications workspace handles the data involved in running its browser-based tools and games.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to app.sergeyromanenko.com, its launcher, its tools and games at root-level routes, and its legal pages. It is a separate document from the privacy policy of sergeyromanenko.com, which covers the main personal website, publications, courses and editorial content. Both domains are operated by the same individual, but they describe different services.

This policy does not cover the practices of third-party services reached from an application or from a link, including YouTube, the exchange-rate provider used by Currency Converter, Google Fonts, or the main website. Those services operate under their own policies.

2. Operator and Contact

The workspace is operated by Sergey Romanenko as a personal project. There is no separate legal entity, company registration or postal address associated with it. The operator acts in a personal capacity.

Questions about this policy or about data practices can be sent to:

awilum.dev@gmail.com

3. Hosting and Delivery

The workspace is built as a static site. Its output is HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WebAssembly modules and public assets. The operator does not run an application server, an upload endpoint, a database, or a first-party analytics database for the workspace. Tool and game state is not sent to an operator-controlled server by the application code.

A static host still has to deliver files. The hosting provider and any CDN in front of it may process technical information such as IP address, requested URL, timestamp, HTTP headers and device metadata as part of serving the site. The operator does not control those systems. This policy does not claim specific retention periods or log handling by the hosting provider, because those details are not established in the project repositories.

4. Information Processed

4.1 No accounts or forms

The workspace has no user accounts, registration, authentication, contact forms, newsletters, checkout, payment processing or comment system. It does not intentionally collect personal information through its own forms, because it has none.

4.2 Technical delivery data

When you request a page or an asset, the hosting infrastructure processes the technical data needed to deliver it. That processing is performed by the provider, not by an application running on the workspace. The operator does not claim to access, analyse or retain those logs beyond whatever the provider does by default.

4.3 Email correspondence

If you email the operator, your email provider and the operator's email provider process your address, message and mail metadata. The website itself does not receive or store that correspondence. Retention follows the email providers' arrangements and the operator's personal email practices; this policy does not promise a fixed deletion period.

4.4 Analytics and tracking

The application shell does not load an analytics beacon. Cloudflare Web Analytics is used on the main website, not on this workspace. No first-party analytics database or visitor profiling system is operated here. This statement describes the application code; it does not describe any logging the hosting provider may perform independently.

5. Legal Bases and Purposes Where GDPR Applies

Whether the GDPR applies to a given visit depends on the operator's establishment and on whether the processing relates to offering services to, or monitoring the behaviour of, people in the EU. The operator does not claim blanket GDPR compliance or non-applicability.

Where the GDPR does apply, the relevant purposes are delivering static pages, running the applications you choose to open, and responding if you write to the operator. Legitimate interests may cover delivery of the site. A contract basis is not used for the workspace, because there are no accounts, purchases or service agreements here. Consent is not collected by the workspace; this is a description of the current implementation, not a claim that no consent could ever be required under applicable law.

A GDPR legal basis is separate from any consent requirement that may arise under cookie or ePrivacy rules. The operator does not assert that every third-party request described below is free of such requirements.

6. Cookies, Tracking and Third-Party Requests

The workspace code does not write cookies and does not contain a tracking pixel or fingerprinting script. That is a statement about the files in this project. It is not a promise that every origin contacted by a page is cookie-free.

Google Fonts is loaded on every page. Google may process technical data and may set its own cookies under its own policy. Currency Converter calls open.er-api.com when you request rates. YouTube on Repeat loads the YouTube iframe API and player. Those providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

A hosting provider may set technical cookies for delivery, security or load balancing. These are outside the operator's control and are not used by the workspace for analytics or profiling. There is no cookie banner on the workspace. If applicable law requires one for the third-party requests above, implementing it is a separate task.

7. Browser-Local Processing and Files

Most applications in the workspace run in your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. Text, code, pasted data and files you select are read into browser memory and processed there. The workspace has no upload endpoint, so selected files are not transmitted to the operator. The result stays on screen or is downloaded when you trigger an export.

This is not true of every application. Currency Converter needs an external exchange-rate service. YouTube on Repeat needs YouTube's player. Their behaviour is described in Section 9. No other application in the workspace is coded to send user-provided content to an external service.

7.1 Files you select or drop

Several tools read local files with the browser's FileReader API. Examples include JSON, YAML, XML, Markdown, images and SQLite database files. The file stays in browser memory for the operation. Closing the tab releases that memory. Downloads you trigger are written by the browser to your device.

7.2 Share links

Several tools offer a share link that encodes the current state into the URL hash fragment, the part after #. Hash fragments are not sent to the host when the page is requested, but anyone with the full link can reconstruct the state. Do not share links that contain confidential material.

7.3 Limits of local processing

Browser-local does not mean risk-free. Browser extensions can read the page, intercept requests and access storage. Malware or forensic tooling on the device may be able to inspect browser memory. The workspace is not audited for regulated environments and makes no security certification. Use your own judgement for sensitive material.

8. Browser Storage and Offline Caches

Some applications remember settings or recent work in localStorage. This is storage on your device, under the app domain, and it is separate from any server-side record. The workspace code does not use IndexedDB or sessionStorage for these applications.

8.1 What is stored

Fifteen applications currently use localStorage:

  • Time Difference Calculator and World Clock store selected city IDs and the time format.
  • Lua Editor stores the last source code, input text and named snippets.
  • YouTube on Repeat stores up to ten recent video IDs, titles and timestamps.
  • 2048 stores board size and best score. Tic-Tac-Toe, Rock Paper Scissors, Simon and Memory Game store settings and scores or best records. Tower of Hanoi and Lights Out store settings and best records per board size.
  • Nim, Catch the Cat and Dots and Boxes store preferences and versioned game data.
  • State Conquest stores preferences, overall statistics and the active match in a versioned save.

These values remain until you clear them in the application where a clear control exists, or through your browser's site-data settings. They are not written to an operator-controlled database.

8.2 Service worker caches

Five games register a service worker: Battleships, Catch the Cat, Dots and Boxes, Nim and State Conquest. The worker caches the game page, the app manifest, hashed bundles and static assets such as images and fonts so the game can load again with less network use. These caches hold application files, not your pasted text, selected files or scores. Scores and settings live in localStorage, which is a different mechanism. You can remove both through your browser's site-data settings.

Offline availability is not guaranteed after a single visit in every browser. It depends on the worker installing successfully and the required assets having been cached. Currency Converter and YouTube on Repeat need a live network connection for their core function.

9. External Services and Network Requests

The workspace contacts the following third-party origins as part of its implementation. Each is governed by its own policy.

Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com)
Loads Instrument Serif, Inter and IBM Plex Mono on every page. Google may process technical request data under its own policy.
open.er-api.com
Currency Converter requests exchange rates from this service. The request includes the base currency code. Amounts, target currencies and results are not part of that request.
YouTube (youtube.com)
YouTube on Repeat loads the YouTube iframe API and embeds a player. The video ID and playback behaviour are handled by YouTube under its own terms and privacy policy.
Same-origin requests and service workers
Pages, bundles and assets are requested from the app domain itself. The five game service workers cache those same-origin files and may revalidate them in the background.

The main website and its support, course and repository links are separate destinations with their own policies. Following such a link takes you off this workspace.

10. Data Retention and Your Rights

Application data you create stays in browser memory or localStorage until you close the tab, clear it in the application, or remove site data in your browser. The operator does not keep a separate copy of that data. Hosting-level technical data is handled by the provider; this policy does not claim control over its retention.

Where the GDPR applies, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and complaint to a supervisory authority. Most of these have limited practical application to data the operator does not hold. For data held by email providers, font or video providers, the exchange-rate service or the hosting provider, contact that provider directly.

To ask about data the operator may actually hold, such as email correspondence, write to awilum.dev@gmail.com .

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the workspace changes. The date at the top records the latest update. Because there are no accounts or notification system, visitors should review the page periodically. Material changes may be noted on the workspace, but no individual notice is promised.
